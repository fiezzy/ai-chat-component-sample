| Bullets | Arial | 11 pt+ | Navy bullets | Regular |

## Logo

- **Square logo** (icon above wordmark) → cover / title slide, square formats
- **Horizontal transparent logo** (icon beside wordmark) → top left on content slides
- Keep clear space around the logo, at least the height of the icon
- Do not recolor, stretch or rotate it
- On dark backgrounds use the transparent / light version

## Slide structure

- Logo top left on every content slide
- Footer: thin dotted line, contact info and slide number
- One topic per slide
- Bullets over long running text
- White background, plenty of space, clean layout

## Writing rules

- No long dashes (—), use short dashes or commas
- Short sentences, active voice
- Highlight key numbers in Hilbi Pink
